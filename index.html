About me

Research interests

01

I am interested in how accumulated experience shapes learning and decision-making.

Two questions I would like to explore are:

What should agents remember—and when should memories be revised?

I am interested in how agents preserve and reuse experience as tasks, tools, and their own capabilities change. Which experiences should remain as detailed episodes, which can become reusable strategies, and when should those memories be revised?

Continual learning · Agent memory · Transfer and adaptation

How should feedback guide decisions as tasks and goals change?

My experience with automated research workflows led me to ask what makes feedback useful for decisions such as exploring, verifying, changing direction, or stopping. I am also interested in how evaluators should adapt when goals change, and recognize when the available evidence or feedback is insufficient.

Reliable feedback · Adaptive evaluation · Sequential decision-making

Selected manuscript

02

First-author manuscript

Stability–Plasticity Theory for Continual Learning under Drifting Task Optima

Xin Huang, Yujing Liu, Fei Zhu, Zhixin Liu, Dacheng Tao

Under review at IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI).

We study a linear learner whose past prediction errors shape the metric used for parameter updates. The analysis establishes conditions for tracking changing task optima with bounded error and separates earlier-task error into current tracking error, task discrepancy, and a learned-geometry interaction term.

A three-task example shows how task order can change the learned metric and retention even when pairwise distances between task optima stay the same.
